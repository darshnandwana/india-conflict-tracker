India Conflict Tracker

Reported security incidents across India, mapped and filterable by region, type, and severity.

Compiled from news and government sources, linked in each incident. Locations are approximate (district level). Figures are as first reported and may be revised. This is a growing selection, not a complete record.

Select an incident on the map or list to see details.

Why India, and why this tracker

Global conflict trackers show India as a few dots among thousands. That hides how different its conflicts are: a border and militancy situation in Jammu & Kashmir, insurgent and ethnic violence in the North East, and a long-running Maoist conflict across central and eastern forest belts.

This tracker looks only at India, so each region can be read on its own terms. It lets students, journalists and researchers see where incidents cluster, what kind they are, and how that changes over time.

Data sources and method

  • Sources: All India Radio News, Business Standard, EastMojo, Al Jazeera, Eurasia Review (which draws on SATP data), and Terrorism Watch. Each incident links to its source.
  • Severity (1 to 5): based on reported deaths. 0-1 deaths is 1 to 2, 2 to 5 is 3, 6 to 10 is 4, more than 10 is 5. When sources give different tolls, the lower figure is used.
  • Limits: reports can be late, disputed or incomplete, and locations are approximate, often to district level.
  • Map: national and state outlines based on Survey of India data via DataMeet, simplified for the web.
Larger dots mean higher severity; pulsing dots are severity 4 or 5.
